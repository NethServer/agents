The delete confirmation is titled with an action, such as `Delete speed dial`, not a question; its body names the speed dial without quotes and says it will be deleted; its buttons are `Cancel` and a danger `Delete`. Fail on `Are you sure…?`, on `Yes`, `OK` or `Confirm`, or on quotes around the name.
